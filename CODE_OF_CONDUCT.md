# Code of Conduct · 行為準則

PinPin follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

PinPin 採用 [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-tw/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)。

## In short · 簡單說

- Be kind and patient; assume good intent · 友善、有耐心，先假設對方是好意
- Critique the work, not the person · 討論作品，不攻擊人
- No harassment, insults, sexualised content or sharing other people's private information
  · 不騷擾、不辱罵、不發布性暗示內容、不公開他人的私人資訊
- Many of us write in a second language — keep it simple and don't mock anyone's English or Chinese
  · 很多人用的不是母語，用簡單的話，不嘲笑別人的英文或中文

## Reporting · 回報

Email **ailazytech@gmail.com** with the subject `[PinPin conduct]`. Reports are kept confidential.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.

寄信到 **ailazytech@gmail.com**，主旨寫 `[PinPin conduct]`，內容會保密。
維護者可以刪除違反準則的留言、commit 或 issue，屢勸不聽的會被封鎖。
